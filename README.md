# Muhammad Aqif Ilyas

**Microbiologist | Antimicrobial Resistance Research | Bacterial Genomics & Bioinformatics**

I am a microbiologist with a research background in antimicrobial resistance, clinical microbiology and molecular diagnostics. My primary research interest lies in investigating the genomic basis of antimicrobial resistance through next-generation sequencing (NGS), bacterial whole-genome sequencing and bioinformatics.

I am particularly interested in understanding the genetic and molecular mechanisms underlying bacterial resistance, their interplay with virulence and biofilm formation, and translating genomic insights into improved surveillance, prevention and the discovery of potential therapeutic alternatives. My broader interests include microbial genomics, molecular epidemiology, vaccine development and alternative antimicrobial strategies.

### Research Interests
- Genomic Epidemiology and Molecular Mechanisms of AMR
- Bacterial Whole-Genome Sequencing and NGS
- Resistance Evolution and Genomic Surveillance
- Biofilm-Associated Resistance and Bacterial Virulence
- Antimicrobial Discovery and Alternative Therapeutic Strategies
- Vaccine Development and Preventive Approaches
